Project Purpose

This dashboard is developed to help illustrate the difference between regular monthly investment and annual lump-sum investment. It can support customer education by presenting calculations, comparisons, scenarios and long-term investment illustrations in an easy-to-understand format.

Potential business use: The dashboard can help staff explain different investment strategies to customers through numerical examples and visual comparisons. It may also support customer understanding of regular investing and long-term investment planning.
  • Compare Person A monthly investment with Person B annual lump-sum investment.
  • Illustrate the effect of different dividend/distribution rates.
  • Show long-term accumulated value and estimated gain.
  • Provide sensitivity analysis for different monthly investment amounts.
  • Provide a simple variable-price fund illustration.
  • Provide a simple educational zakat illustration.
